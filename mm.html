Money Management Tips

Pay yourself first

Move money to savings as soon as you get paid, before spending on anything else.

Build an emergency fund

Aim for 3 to 6 months of living expenses in an easy-to-access account.

Pay off high-interest debt first

Credit cards and similar debts cost the most, so clear them before low-interest loans.

Review your budget monthly

Compare what you planned with what you spent, then adjust for the next month.
